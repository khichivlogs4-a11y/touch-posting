Guest Posting Service

Build authority, visibility and quality backlinks through professional content publishing.

What Is Guest Posting?

Guest posting is the process of writing and publishing an article on someone else's website or blog. It is one of the most effective digital marketing and SEO strategies used to build brand awareness, earn contextual backlinks, and reach relevant target audiences.

How Guest Posting Works

At Touch Posting, we manage the entire outreach and publication cycle securely. We analyze your requirements, identify relevant websites in your niche, prepare engaging content, and secure live placements with natural contextual links.

Key Benefits

  • Contextual Backlinks: Secure relevant links within editorial content.
  • Brand Awareness: Introduce your brand to new industry readers.
  • Referral Traffic: Gain direct visitors from interested audiences.
  • SEO Outreach: Strengthen search engine positioning naturally.

Our Step-by-Step Workflow

Step 1: Requirement Review — We assess your target URLs, anchor texts, and niche goals.
Step 2: Website Matching — We find suitable publisher platforms.
Step 3: Topic Selection — We propose engaging topics aligned with reader interests.
Step 4: Content Preparation — Original articles are written or reviewed.
Step 5: Client Approval — You review the content before publication.
Step 6: Publishing — Article goes live on the target website.
Step 7: Live URL Delivery — We provide you with the permanent live link.

Who Can Use Guest Posting?

Our guest posting service is ideal for SEO agencies, freelancers, SaaS companies, ecommerce businesses, bloggers, local businesses, startups, and digital marketing agencies looking to scale their online authority.

Link Insertions

Add your relevant backlink to an existing article or suitable webpage.

What Is a Link Insertion?

Link insertion (also known as niche edits) involves placing your target backlink into an existing, indexed article on an authoritative website. Because the article is already live and indexed by search engines, link insertions can deliver faster contextual relevance and SEO value.

Why Choose Link Insertions?

  • Existing Authority: Placed on pages that already hold history and indexing.
  • Natural Context: Seamlessly integrated into surrounding paragraphs.
  • Anchor Text Control: Targeted keywords mapped correctly.

Our Link Insertion Process

1. Website Research: Finding relevant pages matching your niche.
2. Article Review: Ensuring content quality and context compatibility.
3. Context Check: Verifying natural placement suitability.
4. Link Placement: Inserting anchor text cleanly.
5. Verification: Confirming live status.
6. Live URL Delivery: Sending you the final link report.
